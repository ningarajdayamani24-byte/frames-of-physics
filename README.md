# Frames of Physics

> A real thought of truth.

Frames of Physics is a personal physics research and learning space for ideas, derivations, visualizations, experiments, and the mathematics behind physical phenomena.

The goal is simple: **do not just present the result — show the frame of thought that leads to it.**

## Direction

- Physics first
- Mathematics without unnecessary decoration
- Visual explanations where they genuinely help
- Interactive simulations when motion makes the idea clearer
- Honest derivations and documented reasoning

## Status

Early development — foundation stage.
